FROM A BLANK WORKSPACE

Tell Mentor about your business.
Build from there.

You don’t need to choose a complete team before you start. Describe your business and let the setup grow around the work you actually need.

  1. 01

    A conversation about your company

    Share what you do, who you serve, your goals and the work that slows you down. Your business brief becomes the team’s shared context.

  2. 02

    A crew with a purpose

    Review suggested employee roles and useful connections. Create the team you need, then adjust names, responsibilities and models as your company changes.

  3. 03

    Work you can follow

    Chat with Mentor or an employee. Assign tasks, link specialists in a workflow, and inspect the owner, brief, result and deliverables.

  4. 04

    A rhythm you control

    Schedule repeat work and operating reports. Set task limits and review rules. External messages and publishing stay in your review queue.
